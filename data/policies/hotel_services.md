# Hotel services

Every hotel offer from Demo Travel Agency (fictional) lists its services. General rules:

- **Check-in / check-out:** check-in from 15:00, check-out before 11:00. Early check-in and late
  check-out depend on availability and may cost extra (usually 20 to 40 EUR).
- **Breakfast:** included only when the offer says "breakfast included"; otherwise it can often be
  added at the hotel (10 to 25 EUR per person and per day).
- **Wi-Fi:** free in all partner hotels.
- **Parking, airport shuttle, pool, gym, spa:** available only where listed in the offer; parking
  and shuttles are usually paid.
- **Families:** children under 6 stay free in their parents' room; cots on request.
- **Accessibility:** accessible rooms exist in hotels that list "accessible rooms"; ask an agent to
  confirm before booking.
- **Pets:** only in hotels listing "pets allowed", usually with a cleaning fee.
- **Taxes:** some cities charge a tourist tax paid at the hotel (about 1 to 5 EUR per person and
  per night).
