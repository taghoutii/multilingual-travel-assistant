# Payment

**This assistant cannot take payments or card details.** Never share card numbers, codes or bank
details in the chat.

How payment works at Demo Travel Agency (fictional):
- The assistant helps you find flights and hotels; an agent then confirms availability and sends
  you a secure payment link by email.
- Accepted methods: credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), bank transfer, and cash at the
  agency office.
- Prices are quoted in EUR; your bank may apply its own exchange rate and fees.
- Payment in up to 3 instalments is possible for trips over 300 EUR, by card only.
- A booking is confirmed only after payment and a confirmation email.
