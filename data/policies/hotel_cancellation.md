# Hotel cancellation

Rules for hotel stays booked through Demo Travel Agency (fictional).

- Each offer shows a **free cancellation deadline** (1, 2, 3 or 7 days before arrival, depending on
  the hotel).
- Cancelling **before** the deadline: free, full refund.
- Cancelling **after** the deadline: the first night is charged; the rest is refunded.
- **No-show** (not arriving without cancelling): the full stay is charged.
- **Non-refundable rates** are cheaper (about 10%) but cannot be cancelled or changed.
- Shortening a stay already started: nights not used are charged unless the hotel agrees otherwise.
- Changing the dates counts as a cancellation followed by a new booking, at the current price.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 days.
- Cancellations are made by an agent (see "Contact an agent").
