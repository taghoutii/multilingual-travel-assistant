# Baggage allowance (flights)

Demo Travel Agency (fictional) sells flights from four partner airlines. Allowances depend on the
airline and are shown on every flight offer.

| Airline | Cabin bag | Checked bags included | Weight per checked bag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demo Air | 1 bag, 8 kg | 1 | 23 kg |
| Skylark Airways | 1 bag, 7 kg | 0 (from 35 EUR per bag, per flight) | 20 kg |
| Meridian Wings | 1 bag, 10 kg | 2 | 23 kg |
| Azure Jet | 1 bag, 8 kg | 1 | 20 kg |

- Cabin bags must fit 55 × 40 × 23 cm. One small personal item (handbag, laptop bag) is also allowed.
- An extra checked bag can be added after booking through an agent: 35 to 60 EUR per bag and per
  flight, depending on the airline.
- Overweight bags (up to 32 kg) cost 50 EUR per bag at the airport.
- Sports equipment and musical instruments must be declared at least 48 hours before departure.
- Liquids in the cabin: containers of 100 ml maximum, in one transparent 1-litre bag.
- Infants (under 2) get one extra checked bag of 10 kg and a folding stroller free of charge.
