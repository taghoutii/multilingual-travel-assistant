# How booking works

The assistant does not make firm bookings. Steps at Demo Travel Agency (fictional):

1. **Search:** ask the assistant for flights and hotels (dates, cities, number of travellers,
   budget). Offers are indicative.
2. **Request:** ask to be put in touch with an agent, or contact the agency directly.
3. **Confirmation:** an agent checks availability and the final price, then sends a quote by email
   (valid 24 hours).
4. **Payment:** you pay through the secure link in the quote (see "Payment").
5. **Documents:** e-tickets and hotel vouchers are sent by email within 24 hours of payment.

Good to know:
- Prices may change until the booking is confirmed.
- Have the names exactly as written in each traveller's passport.
- Changes and cancellations follow the flight and hotel rules.
