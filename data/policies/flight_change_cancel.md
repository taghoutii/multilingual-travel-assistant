# Changing or cancelling a flight

Rules for flights booked through Demo Travel Agency (fictional).

## Changes
- Date or time changes are possible up to 24 hours before departure.
- Change fee: 40 EUR per passenger and per flight, plus any fare difference.
- Changing the passenger name is not possible; minor spelling corrections are free.
- Changes are made by an agent (see "Contact an agent").

## Cancellations
- Within 24 hours of booking, if departure is more than 7 days away: full refund.
- Afterwards: flexible fares are refunded minus a 70 EUR fee per passenger; basic fares are not
  refundable, but airport taxes are always refunded on request.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 days.

## If the airline cancels or delays
- Cancellation by the airline: free rebooking on the next available flight, or a full refund.
- Delay of more than 5 hours: you may cancel for a full refund.
- Passenger rights under local regulations (for example compensation in some regions) also apply.
