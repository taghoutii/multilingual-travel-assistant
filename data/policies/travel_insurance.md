# Travel insurance

Demo Travel Agency (fictional) offers optional travel insurance with its bookings.

| Plan | Price | Main cover |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | 4% of the trip price | cancellation for illness or accident, lost baggage up to 800 EUR |
| Comfort | 6% of the trip price | Basic + medical costs abroad up to 150,000 EUR, repatriation, delays over 6 hours |
| Premium | 8% of the trip price | Comfort + cancellation for any documented reason, baggage up to 2,000 EUR |

- Insurance must be added **at the time of booking** (or at the latest 48 hours after).
- Some destinations or visas require medical insurance: check the embassy website.
- Pre-existing medical conditions are covered only by the Premium plan, on declaration.
- Claims are filed within 30 days of return, with receipts and documents.
- Insurance is not mandatory for booking with the agency.
