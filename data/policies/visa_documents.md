# Visa and travel documents

Demo Travel Agency (fictional) gives general guidance only. **Entry rules change and depend on your
nationality: always check the official website of the embassy or consulate of the country you are
visiting before you travel.** The agency is not responsible for refused entry.

## What to check before any trip
- **Passport validity:** many countries require a passport valid for at least 6 months after your
  return date, with at least one blank page.
- **Visa:** depending on your nationality you may need no visa, an electronic authorization, a visa
  on arrival, or a visa obtained in advance. Processing can take several weeks.
- **Transit:** a transit visa may be needed even if you do not leave the airport.
- **Minors:** children travelling alone or with one parent may need a signed authorization.
- **Health:** some countries require proof of vaccination (for example yellow fever).
- **Return ticket and accommodation:** border officers may ask for proof of both, and of
  sufficient funds.

## What the agency can do
- Provide booking confirmations (flights, hotel) that are often needed for a visa application.
- Point you to the official embassy or consulate website for your destination.
